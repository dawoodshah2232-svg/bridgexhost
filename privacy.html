Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, and what we never do with it. Last updated October 2026.

1. What we collect

Contact details you send us — name, email, company, address, phone — for orders and support. That's it; we don't run accounts, profiles, or tracking pixels that follow you around.

2. Why we collect it

To process domain registrations, run hosting, email and SSL services, bill you, and answer you. Nothing else.

3. What registration requires

Domain registration legally requires sharing registrant details with the registry and registrar. Some of this appears in public WHOIS records, depending on the extension and the privacy options you choose. We'll explain the trade-offs before you commit.

4. What we never do

We never sell your data, rent it, or hand it to marketers. Your details are used to run your services — full stop.

5. Cookies

Our site uses minimal cookies for basic function. No advertising trackers, no cross-site profiling.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored data. Email remains our main channel — please don't send passwords or payment details by email; we'll arrange secure handling when needed.

7. Your rights

Ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or request deletion — subject to registry and legal retention requirements — at contact@bridgingfx.net.
