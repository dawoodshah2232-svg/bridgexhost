Transfers

Bring your domains home.

Moving is simpler than it sounds — and your remaining registration time moves with you.

Four steps

Moving day,
simplified.

Unlock it

Turn off the registrar lock at your current provider. It's usually one toggle in their panel.

Get your code

Ask them for the authorization (EPP) code — it's the password that proves the domain is yours.

Send it over

Email the domain plus the code to contact@bridgingfx.net. Moving several? Send them as a list, one per line.

Approve

Click the confirmation email, and we handle the rest. Your site and email keep running throughout.

Good to know
