About

Domains, done properly.

BridgexHost is the domain arm of Bridging Forex Information Technology LLC in Dubai — built on a simple idea: publish the price, do the work, answer the email.

Why we started BridgexHost

Buying a domain shouldn't feel like a negotiation. Most registrars hide the real price behind coupons, tiers and renewal spikes — so we built the registrar we wanted to buy from.

One published price list. Wholesale cost plus a flat 30%, rounded to .99 — on all 30 of our listed extensions, and quoted the same way for anything else. Free DNS on every domain. Transfers, renewals and nameserver changes handled by people in Dubai who answer.

Our registrations run on ResellerClub's registrar infrastructure — ICANN-accredited backbone serving resellers worldwide — while the relationship stays personal and local.
